## Working configuration

Single-pump system; CentriPro Aquavar CPC; Innomaker isolated USB-RS485 adapter (FTDI FT230X). Live read-only Modbus communication succeeded.

| Dongle terminal | Aquavar X1 terminal |
|---|---|
| 485A (left when looking into wire entries) | 29 (B) |
| GND (middle) | 31 (AGND) |
| 485B (right) | 30 (A) |
| Cable shield, if present | 28 (Screen), drive end only |

The crossed A/B lettering above is the polarity that worked. Swapping the data wires did not work.

**Drive RS485 termination: ON.** Before enabling it, parameter 3208 (EFB UART ERRORS) climbed continuously, even with USB unplugged. Enabling termination stopped that idle error count. With termination enabled and the original polarity restored, all four register blocks read successfully.

The dongle termination switch position was not confirmed. The manual calls for 120-ohm termination at both ends; verify the dongle termination during permanent installation.

## Cat6 installation

- Use both conductors of one twisted pair for the two data lines. Preserve the twist close to the terminals.
- Use a conductor from a different pair for signal reference: dongle GND to drive terminal 31 (AGND).
- Choose and record conductor colors at installation; no color assignment was established during this session.
- Cat6 does not necessarily have a shield. If using shielded Cat6, connect the shield at the drive end only, to terminal 28 (Screen). Do not substitute a signal conductor for a shield.
- Use secure terminals and strain relief suitable for the cable conductors. The temporary jumper setup has only demonstrated communication.
- Before opening or working inside the drive enclosure, power off and wait at least five minutes, per the manual's capacitor-discharge warning.
